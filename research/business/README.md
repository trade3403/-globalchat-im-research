## Vertical portfolio
P0 candidates: paid one-to-one video/text/voice; paid communities. P1 candidates: voice rooms, dating, expert consultation, enterprise messaging, customer support. P2 candidates: live streaming, education, social commerce, secure messaging, AI social. Twelve verticals total (paid text/voice belongs to paid one-to-one).
Reference consumer products: Azar, LivU, HOLLA, Chamet, Yalla, Soul, Hello Yo, BIGO LIVE, Tango, Tinder, Bumble, Discord, Telegram, Patreon, Clarity.fm, JustAnswer, Preply, italki, Slack, Mattermost, Rocket.Chat, Signal, Threema, Element, Replika, Character.AI, Intercom, Crisp, Chatwoot. Research only; do not infer their revenue or licensing from features.

## Shared modules
Wallet/ledger; payments/orders/refunds; server-authoritative metering; gifts; revenue sharing; payouts/reconciliation; subscriptions; guild/agency; matching; booking; commerce; moderation/fraud controls; identity/age verification; financial audit; OEM licensing; currencies/localization.

## Evidence and acceptance
For each vendor distinguish official claim, verified demo, quote, contract and unknown. Capture URL, UTC timestamp, product catalog, frontend/backend/admin source scope, deployment model, license and redistribution rights, price/currency/tax/renewal, third-party RTC dependencies, support SLA and commercial feature coverage. Do not guess revenue, profit, client counts or autonomous technology ownership.
For each vertical document user/admin flows, pages, existing versus new modules, test cases, unit economics and compliance by jurisdiction. Paid calls require consent, transparent prices, server-side metering, idempotent ledger entries, refunds and disputes, payout eligibility, moderation and age safeguards.
Phase BIZ-00 collects public website evidence only. Website access or CI success is not proof of feature availability, pricing, source rights or market demand. No product source changes.
